Why Readable Websites Matter

Good design is not only about appearance. It's also about how easily people understand what they see.

Design for Real People

A website should help visitors find information without unnecessary effort. Clear headings guide readers through the content, while readable paragraphs make ideas easier to understand.

Give Text Room to Breathe

When lines are packed too closely together, readers can struggle to follow them. Thoughtful line spacing creates a more comfortable reading experience, especially when paragraphs contain several sentences.

Good typography makes content feel organized and helps readers stay focused on the message.
